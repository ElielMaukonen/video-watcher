Privacy Policy

Eliel Video Poster · Last updated 2 November 2026

Summary

Eliel Video Poster runs only on the user's own computer. It has no servers and no analytics. It does not sell or share any data, and it sends data only to TikTok.

What data is used

  • TikTok login tokens. After you log in with TikTok, the tool stores the access token, the refresh token and your TikTok open ID in a local file on your own computer. They are used only to call TikTok's API on your behalf.
  • Basic profile information (username, display name and avatar) from TikTok's creator info endpoint. It is shown to you before you post so you can confirm the account. It is not stored.
  • Your videos and captions. They are read from your computer and uploaded directly to TikTok. A local log records which files were posted (file name, caption, privacy setting and TikTok's publish ID) so the same video is not posted twice.

Who receives data

Only TikTok, through its official API, governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy. The author receives no data, and nothing is sent to any third party.

Retention and deletion

All stored data stays on your computer until you delete it. Deleting the tool's local data folder (.tiktok) removes it completely. You can also revoke the tool's access in the TikTok app at any time.

Children

The tool is not directed at children.
